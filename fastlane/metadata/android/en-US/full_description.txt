A private, ad-free weather app built on free NOAA and National Weather Service data. No accounts, no analytics and no API keys built in.

<b>Weather</b>
<ul>
<li>Current conditions, hourly and 7-day forecasts with real numbers: gusts, rain and snow amounts, humidity, cloud cover and thunder chance.</li>
<li>Radar for the past hour, plus a future cast from NOAA's HRRR model adjusted to match the latest radar.</li>
<li>Severe weather alerts in the background for every saved place, with separate channels for warnings, watches, advisories and statements. The Today screen turns red during a warning.</li>
<li>US and Canada: the National Weather Service for the US, Environment and Climate Change Canada for Canada.</li>
<li>Your own WeeWX weather station can supply current conditions.</li>
</ul>

<b>Planning tools</b> (each can be turned on or off)
<ul>
<li>Air quality, UV index and a personal allergy score.</li>
<li>Aurora forecast with notifications when the northern lights may be visible.</li>
<li>Power outage risk, frozen pipes, black ice and frost.</li>
<li>Outdoor activities with the best hours to go, fall foliage, clothes drying, garden, heating, maple sap runs and burn days.</li>
<li>A headline on what matters next ("Rain in about 20 min"), cards you can reorder or hide, and widgets in three sizes.</li>
<li>Storm totals, wildfire smoke, and rivers &amp; tides for paddling.</li>
</ul>

<b>Notifications and widget</b>
<ul>
<li>A home-screen widget with the temperature, conditions and the next rain.</li>
<li>"Rain starting soon" heads-ups and a morning briefing at the time you choose.</li>
</ul>

<b>Backwoods mode</b>
Saves data on satellite connections such as T-Mobile T-Satellite, and while Android's Data Saver is on. You choose exactly what it may still download.

<b>Optional services</b>
Some extras use services that need your own key: Google Pollen API (pollen), EPA AirNow (air quality), OpenWeatherMap (fallback outside North America) and an AI provider (Claude, or your own server) for forecasters' notes, alert explanations and questions. None of them are needed, and nothing is sent to them unless you add a key.

There's also SuperAwesomeMagicalWeather Mini, a separate app for flip phones.
